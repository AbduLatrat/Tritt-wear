Sartorial Standards

Built on Three Pillars of Permanence

Every TRITT garment is produced in measured batches and inspected for collar roll, seam tension, and embroidery density before presentation in our private showroom.

I

Fine-Gauge Knitwear

Spun from long-staple combed cotton and extra-fine merino yarns for breathable structure that resists pilling and holds a sculpted torso line.

II

Architectural Collars

Reinforced Milano-knit spread collars and brushed metal quarter-zip plackets engineered to frame the neckline with quiet authority.

∞

The Infinity Crest

High-density satin stitch embroidery in signature TRITT crimson and obsidian—symbolizing continuity and infinite sartorial possibilities.
